About Me
WHO I AM

As a Senior WordPress Engineer, I specialize in building high-performance, scalable, and visually engaging web solutions that make a real impact.

With deep expertise across Gutenberg, Elementor, and WP Bakery, I've developed custom blocks, widgets, and elements for unique client needs — from in-house plugins and themes to REST APIs for headless WordPress setups and microservices architectures.

I've contributed to high-profile WordPress VIP projects and managed large-scale media publishing platforms with over 4 million monthly visitors, optimizing performance and ensuring seamless content delivery.

Beyond development, I lead and mentor junior developers, oversee design quality, and advise clients on effective digital solutions. I'm passionate about innovation, automation, and contributing to the WordPress community.
